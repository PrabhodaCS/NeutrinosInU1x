The Plan B Model is based on applying an additional
  spontaneously broken  $U(1)_{3B_3-L_1-2L_2}$
  gauge symmetry to the Standard Model (SM) with family dependent
  fermion charges. Right-handed neutrino fields are present in order to cancel gauge anomalies. The model was proposed
  to ameliorate fits of the SM to observables 
  involving the $b\rightarrow s l^+ l^-$ transition whilst simultaneously
  explaining the relatively large mass of the third family of quarks, compared
  to the first two. 
  We investigate its implications for neutrino masses and mixing angles.
  The \model\ naturally implements the Froggatt-Nielsen mechanism for neutrino
  mass and mixing hierarchies via a Type-I see-saw mechanism which leads to an
  inverted hierarchy  pattern which agrees
  with those inferred from neutrino oscillations. A normal hierarchy requires an additional percent-level suppression of a Yukawa coupling.
